Model Builder

Build financial statement workbooks and valuation templates from company filings.

Last updated September 18, 2026

On this page

  • Build configuration
  • 3 Statement Model
  • Valuation templates

Build configuration #

Search by company name or ticker, then configure the workbook.

Model

The 3 Statement Model includes the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement. Add Discounted Cash Flow for WACC and valuation tables, or Leveraged Buyout for transaction assumptions, sources and uses, an operating case, a debt schedule, and sponsor returns.

Build mode & periods

AA combines annual historical periods with annual forecast columns. IAIA includes interim and annual historical periods, with quarterly forecasts before annual forecasts.

Choose 3, 5, or 10 historical years and 1–20 forecast years. In IAIA mode, set how many forecast years include quarterly periods.

Formatting & output

Set the header color, Bold subtotals, or Split by statement for separate statement worksheets. Selecting a DCF or LBO template disables Split by statement.

Preview

The preview reflects your configuration, with placeholder rows and values.

3 Statement Model #

Contains formatted financial statements including the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement. Historical figures are derived from XBRL data in the company’s SEC filings and organized across the selected historical periods. The workbook also includes forecast columns for your projections.

Figures that could not be merged across periods are retained at the bottom of the relevant statement in the red Unmerged / Needs Review section.

Valuation templates #

Select Discounted Cash Flow or Leveraged Buyout in the build configuration to include its template alongside the three-statement model.

Discounted Cash Flow

A template for cash-flow projections, WACC, and valuation.

Leveraged Buyout

A template for transaction assumptions, sources and uses, an operating case, a debt schedule, and sponsor returns.
