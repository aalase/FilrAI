Getting started

Find a company, explore its filings, and follow new disclosures.

Last updated September 11, 2026

On this page

  • Installation & requirements
  • Choose a company
  • Explore a filing
  • Follow the company

Installation & requirements #

Install from the Mac App Store. Requires macOS 15.6 or later.

An internet connection is required to load filings, market data, and AI features.

Choose a company #

Search by company name or ticker in Overview to view its business overview and financials. More about Overview.

Explore a filing #

Open a filing in the Viewer, or select a source citation in the company overview to jump directly to the supporting passage.

Follow the company #

Add the company to a watchlist and use the Tracker to follow new filings. More about filing tracking.
