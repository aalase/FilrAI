Privacy Policy

How FilrAI handles app data, cloud AI requests, local storage, third-party services, and support communications.

Effective date: May 9, 2026

FilrAI is a macOS app for researching SEC filings, company disclosures, investor materials, and AI-assisted summaries. This Privacy Policy explains what information FilrAI handles and when information may leave your device.

Information FilrAI Handles

Depending on the features you use, FilrAI may handle:

  • Company searches, ticker symbols, filing references, and research questions.
  • Notes, annotations, highlighted text, uploaded PDFs, and app preferences.
  • Prompts, filing excerpts, document context, and generated outputs when you use AI-assisted features.
  • A user-provided identifier, such as a user agent, used to access third-party data sources including the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.
  • Your email address and message contents if you contact support.

Local Storage

FilrAI does not require user accounts. App settings, cached filing data, notes, annotations, uploaded documents, and preferences are stored locally on your device unless a feature clearly involves an external service.

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it through the app, remove the files through macOS, or uninstall FilrAI.

Cloud AI Features

Some FilrAI features use cloud-based AI services. When you use those features, prompts, selected filing excerpts, document context, questions, and generated outputs may be sent to FilrAI cloud services and third-party AI model providers to process the request.

Cloud services may process, log, or retain submitted data according to their API data policies, security practices, and service settings. Please do not submit account credentials, private financial records, confidential company information, material non-public information, or sensitive personal information unless you are comfortable with that information being processed by cloud services.

Third-Party Services and Data Sources

FilrAI may connect to third-party services to provide app functionality, including:

  • SEC systems, public filing sources, company investor-relations websites, and market-information sources.
  • Cloud AI model providers used to generate AI-assisted responses.
  • Email and hosting providers used to operate this website and respond to support requests.

Third-party services process information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide app functionality, retrieve public filing and company information, generate AI-assisted responses, troubleshoot issues, respond to support requests, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

FilrAI does not sell personal information, does not use advertising frameworks, and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. FilrAI does not currently use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. If these practices change, this policy will be updated before those features are used.

Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it. Support emails may be retained as needed to respond to requests and maintain ordinary business records. Cloud AI providers and other third-party services may retain request data according to their own policies and service configurations.

You can contact support to ask privacy questions or request deletion of support communications associated with your email address.

Children's Privacy

FilrAI is not directed to children under 13, and children under 13 should not use FilrAI or submit information through the app. FilrAI does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used FilrAI, please contact support with questions about any information that may be associated with support communications.

Security

FilrAI uses reasonable safeguards, including encrypted network connections where supported by third-party services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please use care when deciding what to submit.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as FilrAI features, vendors, or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are made.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: filr.support@gmail.com.
