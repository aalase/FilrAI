Privacy Policy

Effective: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the collection, use, storage, and disclosure of information by FilrAI.

Information Collected

The information processed depends on the features used and includes:

  • Searches, watched companies, and research questions.
  • Documents, notes, highlights, preferences, and generated responses.
  • Subscriber IDs, purchase records, and subscription status.
  • Feature usage, service costs, and technical information such as errors, performance, and IP addresses.
  • Email addresses, messages, and attachments provided to support.

FilrAI does not require a separate login. Subscription and cloud services use an internal subscriber ID associated with Apple purchase records.

Data Storage and Processing

Documents, notes, and settings are stored on your Mac. When AI features are used, relevant searches, questions, document text, notes, and conversation history are sent through Cloudflare to OpenAI for processing. Document chat may transmit the full text of a document. Processing may begin automatically when a feature is opened or used.

Company selections for briefings are stored on our servers. Briefings may be prepared while the app is closed.

Use of Information

We use information to provide features, verify subscriptions, manage usage and costs, understand feature use, maintain reliability and security, provide support, and meet legal obligations.

Usage reports are linked to your subscriber ID but exclude the content of searches, documents, and conversations. This content is processed separately to provide the requested service. Service providers also keep technical logs for reliability, security, and abuse prevention.

FilrAI does not sell personal information, display advertisements, or use information for tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Third-Party Services

Apple processes purchases. Cloudflare hosts our services and stores subscriber, usage, and briefing records. OpenAI processes content submitted for search assistance and generated responses.

Market-data, news, and public research providers receive company queries, requested symbols, and network information. Website hosts process connection information, and email providers process support messages and attachments.

Providers processing data for FilrAI must protect it consistently with this policy and applicable law. Their own privacy policies also apply.

Data Retention and Deletion

Usage records expire after limited reporting periods. Subscriber, subscription, and briefing records do not currently expire automatically. Accounting and support records may remain longer for business, security, or legal needs.

Files uploaded for document chat and their search indexes are configured to expire automatically. Closing a chat does not necessarily delete them immediately. OpenAI may retain submitted content for abuse prevention for up to 30 days by default, with exceptions described in its retention policy.

You can manage local data through FilrAI and macOS. Uninstalling may leave files or backups and does not delete cloud records. To stop further processing by these services, discontinue use of AI features and remove companies selected for cloud briefings.

Contact us to request deletion of cloud or support data. We may need to verify the request and locate your records. Some records may be retained for legal, security, or accounting reasons. Deleting data does not cancel your subscription; manage subscriptions through Apple.

Children's Privacy

FilrAI is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Data Security

We encrypt data in transit. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy and its effective date. Material changes will be announced in the app or on this website.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email filr.support@gmail.com.
